# Multi-Sport Facility Email Pitch

## Email Version

```text
Subject: Online booking system for your courts

Good day po,

I'm Paolo, a web developer. I recently built an online booking and management system for sports facilities, and I think it could be useful for facilities with multiple court types like basketball, pickleball, badminton, and volleyball.

The system allows customers to choose a court, select an available date and time, submit their booking details, and upload payment proof online. On the admin side, the owner or staff can manage reservations, review payment proof, update booking status, and check court schedules in one dashboard.

This can help reduce manual booking messages, organize payment screenshots, avoid double bookings, and make the reservation process more professional for customers.

It can also be customized with your facility name, logo, court types, pricing, available schedules, and payment details.

Thank you po!

Paolo
```
