# Sports Facility Owner Pitch

## Positioning

Do not pitch HoopsBookingApp as only a basketball booking website. Pitch it as a practical booking and operations system for sports facility owners.

Core message:

> This is an online booking and management system for sports courts and facilities. It helps customers book available schedules online, submit payment proof, and receive booking confirmation, while the owner or admin manages reservations, schedules, payment verification, and booking status in one dashboard.

The strongest selling angle is not technical novelty. It is less manual admin work, fewer booking mistakes, better payment tracking, and a more professional customer experience.

## Buyer

The first buyer is usually not the player/customer. The buyer is more likely to be:

- A sports facility owner
- An operator or manager
- Staff handling bookings manually
- A small court business using Facebook Messenger, phone calls, screenshots, or spreadsheets

## Owner Pain Points

Lead with the problems they already understand:

- Too many booking messages to track manually
- Payment screenshots mixed into chat threads
- Risk of double bookings or missed schedule updates
- Hard to know which bookings are pending, confirmed, paid, cancelled, or completed
- Customers repeatedly asking for available time slots
- Staff spending time confirming the same details again and again

## Value Proposition

HoopsBookingApp helps a facility:

- Let customers book available schedules online
- Track guest and member reservations
- Collect payment proof from customers
- Review and verify payments from an admin dashboard
- Manage booking status clearly
- Reduce back-and-forth messages
- Avoid schedule conflicts
- Present a more professional booking process
- Customize the system with the facility name, logo, courts, and payment details

## Demo Script: 60 to 90 Seconds

Use this for a screen recording or quick live demo.

### Opening

```text
Good day po. This is a quick demo of the booking system I built for sports courts and facilities.

The goal is to help facilities reduce manual booking work, organize payment verification, and make the reservation process easier for customers.
```

### Step 1: Show The Customer Booking Page

Action:

- Open the public booking page.
- Point out the facility name, logo, available courts, date selection, and time slots.

Script:

```text
On the customer side, they can choose the court, date, and available time slot directly from the website. This helps reduce repeated messages asking what schedules are still available.
```

### Step 2: Select A Court, Date, And Time

Action:

- Select a court.
- Select a date.
- Select one or more available time slots.

Script:

```text
Once the customer chooses a schedule, the system prepares the booking summary so they can review the details before submitting.
```

### Step 3: Show Customer Details And Payment Option

Action:

- Show customer information fields.
- Show payment instructions or payment proof submission.

Script:

```text
The customer can enter their name and contact details, then submit their payment proof. This keeps the reservation and payment screenshot connected to the same booking instead of being buried in chat.
```

### Step 4: Show Booking Confirmation

Action:

- Submit or show an existing successful booking.
- Show booking reference/status if available.

Script:

```text
After submission, the customer gets a confirmation page or booking reference. This makes the process feel more official and gives both sides a clearer record.
```

### Step 5: Show Admin Dashboard

Action:

- Switch to the admin dashboard.
- Show reservation list, payment status, and booking status.

Script:

```text
On the admin side, the owner or staff can see bookings in one dashboard. They can check which reservations are pending, confirmed, paid, cancelled, completed, or still needing payment verification.
```

### Step 6: Show Payment Verification

Action:

- Open a booking or transaction with payment proof.
- Show approve/reject or review action if available.

Script:

```text
For payments, the admin can review the submitted proof and update the booking status. This helps organize GCash, Maya, bank transfer, or other payment workflows that are usually handled manually.
```

### Step 7: Show Calendar Or Schedule View

Action:

- Show the calendar/schedule page.
- Point out how confirmed bookings and blocked schedules appear.

Script:

```text
The schedule view helps staff see occupied slots and avoid double bookings. It can also support blocked schedules for maintenance, private events, or other unavailable times.
```

### Closing

```text
The system can be customized for your facility's name, logo, courts, prices, and payment details.

It is not just a website. It is a lightweight operations tool to help manage bookings, payment verification, and schedules more professionally.
```

## What To Avoid In The First Message

Avoid leading with:

- Too many technical details
- AI features
- Database, Supabase, React, or Vite
- A long feature list
- A hard sales message
- Pricing before they understand the value

The first goal is only to get permission to send a demo.

## Best Demo Flow

The most persuasive order is:

1. Customer books a schedule.
2. Customer submits payment proof.
3. Admin sees the booking.
4. Admin verifies payment.
5. Admin checks schedule/calendar.
6. Explain customization for their facility.

## Practical Closing Question

After showing the demo, ask:

```text
Would this kind of system help reduce the manual booking and payment checking work for your facility?
```

This is better than immediately asking if they want to buy. It invites them to talk about their actual workflow and pain points.

## Should This Be Offered For Free?

Do not give the full app away for free forever by default. A better approach is to offer a free demo or short free pilot, then discuss a paid setup or monthly arrangement if the facility finds it useful.

Recommended offer:

> Free demo + free short pilot, then paid monthly if it helps their operations.

This keeps the first conversation low-risk for the owner while still protecting the value of the work.

### Option 1: Free 2-Week Pilot

Offer to customize the system for their facility for a limited trial period.

Include:

- Facility name and logo
- Courts or spaces
- Basic prices
- Payment details
- Admin access
- Sample or real booking flow

After the pilot, ask if they want to continue with a monthly arrangement.

### Option 2: Discounted First Client

For an early partner, offer a lower starting price in exchange for:

- Feedback
- Permission to use the project as a case study
- A testimonial if they are happy with it
- Referrals to other facility owners

This is useful if the first client can help prove the product in the real world.

### Option 3: Free Demo Only

The demo is free, but setup and customization are paid.

This is cleaner if the owner already understands the value or wants a production-ready version immediately.

### Avoid Free Forever

Avoid offering the system free forever unless there is a strategic reason.

Only consider free forever if the facility can give something valuable in return, such as:

- Strong testimonial
- Real usage data and feedback
- Public case study permission
- Introductions to other facility owners
- A strong portfolio reference

## Simple Payment Plan After Trial

Keep the first paid offer simple. Do not create too many packages at the beginning. One clear starter plan is easier for a facility owner to understand.

Recommended starter range:

```text
Setup fee: PHP 3,000 - PHP 5,000 one-time
Monthly fee: PHP 1,000 - PHP 2,500 per month
```

What the starter plan can include:

- Facility branding: name, logo, and basic colors if needed
- Court setup and pricing setup
- Public booking page
- Admin dashboard
- Payment proof tracking
- Basic support
- Small updates
- Hosting and app maintenance

## First-Client Offer

For the first real client, make the offer easier so there is less friction.

Suggested first-client price:

```text
Setup fee: PHP 3,000 one-time
Monthly fee: PHP 1,000 per month after the trial
```

Use this when the first client can provide useful feedback, a testimonial, or a real-world case study.

## Standard Future Offer

After the system is proven with an early client, future clients can be priced higher.

Suggested standard price:

```text
Setup fee: PHP 5,000 one-time
Monthly fee: PHP 1,500 - PHP 2,500 per month
```
